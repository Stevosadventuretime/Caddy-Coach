3D golfer's body and clothes (golfer-mesh.wasm and the other golfer-*.jpg / golfer-hair.png textures)
  Body shape, skeleton, skin weights and eyes come from MakeHuman (makehumancommunity.org),
  released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). The clothes, shoes, cap, hair, textures, grip and
  posing were made for Caddy Coach.

Real swing motion (swing-model.json)
  The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding
  from NSF EIA-0196217. (CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 64; adjusted for Caddy Coach.)

3D library
  three.js, MIT licence.

Hole flyover
  Satellite imagery: Esri World Imagery (Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics), shown with attribution.
  Ground heights: Copernicus DEM via Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0).

Course maps (holes, greens and tees)
  Course data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0):
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  The shared hole maps in Caddy Coach are built partly from OpenStreetMap data and partly from golfers' own mapping.
