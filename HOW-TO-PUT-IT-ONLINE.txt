CADDY COACH: STANDALONE APP
===========================

This folder is the whole Caddy Coach app. Put it on a free web host and it runs on its own
web address, where your phone's GPS, satellite maps and live course data all work.

DO THIS FIRST: LOGINS AND PASSWORDS (once, about 5 minutes, BEFORE you upload this version)
Every golfer now has their own login with a strong password, and each username can only be used once.
1. Open your Supabase project (supabase.com > your project).
2. Authentication > Sign In / Providers > Email:
   - turn OFF "Confirm email"
   - set "Minimum password length" to 10
   - Save.
3. SQL Editor > New query. Open caddy-coach-supabase-setup.sql, copy all of it, paste it in, press Run.
   It should say "Success. No rows returned". It's safe to run again.
4. Now upload this version (below).
What testers see: the next time anyone who signed up with the old 1234 password logs in, they are asked
to choose their own password. It must be at least 10 characters, with upper and lower case letters,
at least 2 numbers and a special character (like ! ? # %), and not be a common or easy-to-guess one.
Once everyone has done that (check in SQL Editor: select username from profiles where auth_id is null;
it should return nothing), run step 7 at the bottom of the SQL file to close the old route for good.
Emails and forgotten passwords: everyone gives an email address when they sign up or choose their new password
(people who signed up before are asked for one the next time they open the app). It's kept with their login, never
shown to other golfers, and used only for "Forgotten your password?" on the log-in screen. For the reset emails to
reach golfers, do these two things once in Supabase:
 a) Authentication > URL Configuration: set "Site URL" to https://caddycoach-test.netlify.app
    and add the same address under "Redirect URLs". The link in the email brings people back there.
 b) Authentication > Emails > SMTP Settings: turn on custom SMTP. Without it Supabase only emails members of your
    Supabase team. The easiest free option is a Gmail account with an app password:
    host smtp.gmail.com, port 465, username = the Gmail address, password = an app password
    (Google Account > Security > 2-Step Verification > App passwords). Sender email = the same Gmail address,
    sender name = Caddy Coach. For a public launch, use a proper email service (e.g. Resend) with your own domain.

TWO SETTINGS TO FILL IN (in index.html, search for them; both are optional for testing)
- APP_OWNER_EMAIL='' : put the email address golfers should contact about their data. It appears
  in the privacy notice. Needed before you launch publicly.
- WEATHER={provider:'open-meteo',key:''} : the free Open-Meteo service is for non-commercial use.
  Before you charge for the app or advertise it, buy an Open-Meteo API plan (open-meteo.com/en/pricing)
  and paste the key in, or switch provider to 'openweathermap' with an OpenWeatherMap key.

PUT IT ONLINE (about 5 minutes, free)
1. On a computer, go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up for a free account.
2. Unzip CaddyCoach-Standalone.zip, then drag the whole "caddy-coach" folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a web address like https://something-random.netlify.app.
   Under Site configuration > Change site name you can make it e.g. https://caddycoach-test.netlify.app

INSTALL IT ON YOUR PHONE
- iPhone: open the address in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
It opens full screen with the Caddy Coach icon. Once it has opened online, it also works
without signal (the satellite map needs signal the first time you view each course).

UPDATING
In Netlify, open your site, go to Deploys, and drag the new "caddy-coach" folder onto the
deploy area. The web address stays the same and the app updates next time you open it.

HOW IT WORKS WITHOUT CLAUDE
- Courses: every UK course on OpenStreetMap is found automatically and quickly, near your
  GPS position or by name. Results are remembered, so the second time is instant.
- Greens: where a course's holes are numbered on the map, every green is matched to its hole
  automatically. Where the greens are drawn but not numbered, the app matches the green to
  the hole using your position and the card length, and confirms it when you walk onto the
  green; the rest show as white flags with your distance to each. Matches are saved, so each
  course is fully set up after one round.
- Hole map: satellite view, your position, live yards to the middle of the green, tap to measure.
- Swing feedback: Caddy Coach's own coach writes your summary, strengths, top priority and
  a finding and fix for every area, on the phone, with no internet needed.
- Saved swings, clips, rounds and course cards are kept on the phone. Your clubs and yardages are also saved with your profile, so they come back wherever you log in.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Some smaller courses haven't had their holes mapped on OpenStreetMap yet. For those the app
  says so, and you set the card and greens once. Anyone can add a course's holes at
  openstreetmap.org, and the app picks them up automatically.
- Saved swings and rounds stay on the phone they were made on. Your profile, clubs, handicap
  record, leaderboard posts, group rounds and competitions are online, so they follow your login.
- Feedback sent from the app (Me > Send feedback) lands in Supabase: Table Editor > course_cards,
  rows whose course_key starts with fb~.

NEW IN THIS VERSION
- Group rounds: on the course screen, "Start a group" gives a 5-character code. Friends enter it on
  their own phone before they tee off, and everyone sees each other's scores update during the round.
- Club competitions: Play > Club > Competitions. Anyone at a club can set one up (Stableford or
  net medal, up to 3 months). Rounds posted to the club leaderboard between the dates count.
- Goals and a getting-started checklist on Home, and an Updates card (handicap changes, goals reached,
  practice reminders, new rounds and competitions at your club).
- Phone notifications when a swing analysis or shot trace finishes while you're in another app
  (Me > Text size and display). On iPhone this works once the app is added to the Home Screen.
  Notifications sent when the app is closed (true push) need a small server; see APP STORES below.
- Course rating, slope and stroke index fill in automatically when other golfers have entered them
  for those tees, with a note saying how many golfers they came from.
- The shot tracer now says what the ball flight means for your clubface and swing path, and the
  swing report uses your last traced shot for its clubface and path section.

LIVE PINS, CLUBS, COACHING AND CADDY COACH PLUS
- Live pins: when a golfer is near a green, the hole map asks "Where's the hole today?". One mark shows as
  "not confirmed"; once two golfers agree (within 4 m) it's "confirmed", and everyone gets yards to the pin.
  Pins clear at midnight. A club's own pin sheet always wins.
- Club admins: you (stevosadventuretime) are the app owner. In the app: Me > App usage and club admins, type a
  golfer's username and press "Make club admin". They run their local course: a club page (logo, colour,
  welcome message) on the Club tab, official competitions, and "Set today's pins" for the greenkeeper or pro,
  with a printable pin sheet.
- App usage: Me > App usage and club admins shows golfers per week, rounds, swings, pins and how many come back
  after four weeks. It's anonymous: golfers are counted by a one-way code, not their name.
- Coaching: Me > Coaching. A coach taps "Get my coach code"; a pupil enters it, then uses "Send to my coach" under
  any swing (scores and key-position pictures, never the video). The coach sends notes back; pupils see them in
  Coaching and as an update on Home.
- Weather: comes through Caddy Coach's server function. To switch to the Met Office (free for up to 360 lookups a
  day, and answers are shared between golfers for 30 minutes): register at datahub.metoffice.gov.uk, subscribe to
  "Site Specific Global Spot" (free plan), then in Supabase > Edge Functions > Secrets add METOFFICE_API_KEY with
  your key. Until then it uses Open-Meteo, which is free for non-commercial use only.
- Caddy Coach Plus (built, switched off). Free: GPS, scoring, stats, handicap, leaderboards, 3 swing analyses a
  month. Plus: unlimited swing analysis, shot tracer, live grip coaching, swing comparison. To switch it on, once
  you're set up as a sole trader or company:
   1. Create a free Stripe account. Add two products (e.g. GBP 4.99 monthly, GBP 34.99 yearly, 14-day free trial)
      and a Payment Link for each.
   2. Stripe > Developers > Webhooks: add endpoint
      https://dhrzscyirgvjwnvgdpqt.supabase.co/functions/v1/cc-stripe-webhook
      with events checkout.session.completed and customer.subscription.created/updated/deleted. Copy its
      signing secret into Supabase > Edge Functions > Secrets as STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET.
   3. In index.html set PLUS={on:true, linkMonthly:'<monthly link>', linkYearly:'<yearly link>',
      portal:'<Stripe customer portal link>', ...}. Or ask Claude to do it.

TESTING ON REAL PHONES
Before a wider launch, get 5 to 10 golfers with a mix of phones (at least two iPhones, one older
Android, one newer Android) to use it for two weeks. Send them the web address; "Invite a friend"
under Send feedback shares it. Ask them to use Send feedback for anything odd.

APP STORES (when you're ready)
The app is set up as an installable web app (icons, maskable icon, screenshots, offline support).
- Google Play: go to https://www.pwabuilder.com, enter your Netlify address, choose Android, and
  download the package. You need a Google Play developer account (one-off fee). PWABuilder gives you
  an assetlinks.json file: put it in a folder called .well-known inside caddy-coach and upload again.
- Apple App Store: on PWABuilder choose iOS to get an Xcode project. You need a Mac with Xcode and
  an Apple Developer account (yearly fee). Apple reviews apps that are mostly a website wrapper more
  strictly, so expect to explain the camera, GPS and analysis features in the review notes.
- Before either store: fill in APP_OWNER_EMAIL, put the privacy notice on a public web page (stores
  ask for its address), buy the weather licence, and switch the satellite maps to your own ArcGIS key.
- True push notifications (for example "your friend posted a round" when the app is closed) need a
  Supabase Edge Function plus Web Push keys, or Firebase Cloud Messaging inside the store apps.
