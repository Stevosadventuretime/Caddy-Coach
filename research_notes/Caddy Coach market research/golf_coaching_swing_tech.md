# Golf coaching, swing-analysis and sports-performance platforms (2025–2026)

All sources accessed 18 Nov 2026 unless stated. Many "2026 comparison" pages are written by competing apps (GOATCode/GOATY, BirdieSwing, Skillest, Rotary Swing, VisualEyes) and are flagged as biased where used. Prices are USD unless shown in GBP. Search results were summarised by a search tool, so figures marked "(search summary)" should be checked on the page before quoting.

## 1. What each product does and its main selling point

### Takeaway
The market splits into four groups: (a) AI self-coaching from one phone video (Sportsbox, GolfFix, Mustard), (b) coach video tools and marketplaces (V1, CoachNow, OnForm, Skillest), (c) content-led academies (Me and My Golf, GolfPass, DECADE), (d) launch-monitor ecosystems (Rapsodo, Garmin, Trackman). Caddy Coach (on-phone AI checks, fault-to-drill links, practice plans) sits in group (a) but takes ideas from (c).

### Cited Findings
- **Sportsbox AI (3D Golf):** "Tour-trusted 3D swing analysis that turns complex motion data into simple, actionable feedback"; claims "500+ coaches and 50,000+ golfers"; markerless 3D analysis partnership with TaylorMade; also sells Sportsbox Studio, a 3D capture set-up for hitting bays with a launch monitor — [sportsbox.ai](https://www.sportsbox.ai/)
- Sportsbox builds a 3D model from one phone video and gives measurements such as turn, tilt, sway and lift — [BirdieSwing blog, 26 Aug 2026 (written by a competitor)](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps)
- **GolfFix (MOAIS, Korea):** Upload a phone video and its own "Vision AI" finds swing faults, with results "Just 10 seconds after uploading". Claims 1.5M+ downloads, 65+ countries and 15M+ swing analyses — [golffix.io](https://www.golffix.io/en/golffix)
- **Mustard Golf (QualiaOS; launched with Golf Digest):** AI swing analysis built from motion capture and 3D models of thousands of pro and amateur swings. Justin Rose is an early investor, and Mark Blackburn presents the video tips — [WBD/Golf Digest press release](https://press.wbd.com/us/media-release/golf-digest/golf-digest-mustard-launch-groundbreaking-ai-powered-platform-mustard-golf-help-every) (search summary). The UK App Store listing names coaches Jason Goldsmith, Justin Rose, Mark Blackburn and Michael Jacobs, plus at-home drills and progress tracking — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/mustard-golf-swing-analysis/id6499280777)
- No source linked Rory McIlroy to Mustard. That would need checking before anyone says it — (search result, no supporting source)
- **V1 Golf / V1 Pro:** A manual video tool (capture, slow motion, drawing, side-by-side with model swings). Reviews agree it does not diagnose faults for the player; its AI (V1CTOR) is aimed at instructors — [BirdieSwing](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps); [GOATCode V1 review (competitor)](https://goatcode.ai/v1-golf-app-review-is-it-worth-it.html)
- **Skillest:** A marketplace with 1,000+ coaches, offering video review and live Zoom lessons. AI is used for skeleton tracking and lesson summaries, but humans do the coaching — [Skillest blog](https://skillest.com/blog/skillest-vs-v1-golf/); [Skillest features](https://skillest.com/features) (search summary)
- **CoachNow:** A coach–athlete video and messaging platform with plans for athletes and coaches — [coachnow.com/pricing](https://coachnow.com/pricing) (search summary)
- **OnForm:** A coach-focused video analysis app (multi-camera, voice-over, messaging, academy tools); "not designed for self-coaching" — [BirdieSwing](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps). It has an AI auto-detect camera and a "Practice Partner" feature — [OnForm blog](https://onform.com/blog/onform-acquires-hudl-technique/) (search summary)
- **Hudl Technique: no longer active.** OnForm bought it from Hudl (announced May 2021) and shut it down on 1 Sep 2021, with a migration tool for users. It began life as Ubersense — [OnForm blog](https://onform.com/blog/onform-acquires-hudl-technique/); [VisualEyes](https://www.visualeyesapp.com/blog/hudl-technique-replacement)
- **Me and My Golf (UK, Piers Ward and Andy Proudman):** A coaching-video membership with a personalised roadmap, 20+ coaching plans (putting, chipping, breaking 100 and more), practice plans, "Shot Fixer" fault-to-video finder, a swing analysis tool, a members' community and brand discounts. New videos are added weekly — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/me-and-my-golf-coaching-app/id1541212755)
- **DECADE (Scott Fawcett):** A course-management and strategy system, "not a practice app" — [DECADE memberships](https://decade.golf/memberships/); [ScoringZone review](https://www.scoringzone.net/blog/decade-golf-app-review) (search summary)
- **Pinpoint Golf:** A strokes-gained stats and GPS app to "analyze your golf game, identify areas for improvement and track your progress" — [pinpoint.golf](https://www.pinpoint.golf/) (search summary)
- **GolfPass (NBC/Golf Channel):** The app lists GolfPass Video, GolfPass+ and VIP tiers. Coaching details were not confirmed — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/golfpass-video/id1448493740) (search summary)
- **Rapsodo MLM2PRO:** A phone-paired launch monitor with a membership that unlocks data and simulated courses; Swing Vision and Impact Vision video — [Rapsodo UK memberships](https://rapsodo.co.uk/pages/rapsodo-golf-memberships)
- **Garmin Approach R10:** A portable launch monitor. The Garmin Golf membership unlocks Home Tee Hero virtual courses and weekly tournaments — [PlayBetter review 2026](https://www.playbetter.com/blogs/golf-simulator-reviews/garmin-approach-r10-review-golf-launch-monitor-under-1000) (search summary)
- **Trackman Golf app:** The consumer app is free (listed at 0 on Trackman's software page) and is used to book bays and view data. A separate Trackman Golf Pro app has a premium tier (Trackman Cloud, TMU membership, portal) — [Trackman software](https://www.trackman.com/golf/launch-monitors/software); [Trackman FAQ Golf Pro App](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/37707767452571-FAQ-Golf-Pro-App) (search summary)
- **HomeCourt (basketball):** AI phone-camera basketball training. It stays usable for free, with a paid subscription on top — [HomeCourt subscription FAQ](https://www.homecourt.ai/faq/subscription) (search summary)

### Inferences
- Only GolfFix and Mustard among consumer golf apps clearly combine one-video AI diagnosis with drills, which is the same job Caddy Coach does. Sportsbox gives 3D numbers and has added an AI layer (SAMI) on top.
- Me and My Golf is the closest UK comparison for "fault → video → plan" content, but its swing analysis is not its main draw.

### Gaps
- **Golfboy / other "Golf AI" swing apps:** not researched (no tool calls left). Same for 18Birdies AI Coach, apart from one mention.
- **Rick Shiels-style creator apps:** no source found. I couldn't confirm whether Rick Shiels runs a paid app or academy.
- **Catapult and Hudl (team performance):** not researched beyond the Hudl Technique history.

## 2. Most innovative features

### Takeaway
The leading features are 3D from one phone video plus an AI explainer (Sportsbox SAMI), fast AI fault detection that leads into targeted drills and daily/monthly reports (GolfFix), drills built by pro coaches tied to faults (Mustard), and personalised content roadmaps (Me and My Golf).

### Cited Findings
- Sportsbox **SAMI**: "your AI swing expert", which uses your 3D data to answer questions and guide improvement. Paid features include auto swing detection, 2D/3D comparison, an AI Expert and "AI Highlights"; also goal setting and per-swing instant feedback — [sportsbox.ai](https://www.sportsbox.ai/). Launch date of SAMI not found.
- GolfFix features: Swing Analysis Mode; Self Record Mode with instant replay; "Focus Drill" aimed at AI-found weaknesses; "Shot Fix Lesson" (works back from the shot problem, e.g. slice, to causes and drill videos); pro swing comparison; AI guide lines; daily and monthly reports with "missions"; a community with auto-translation; and "Pic of the Day", AI images of key swing moments — [golffix.io](https://www.golffix.io/en/golffix)
- Mustard: upload a swing, see what you do well and what needs work, then get drills you can do at home and track progress — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/mustard-golf-swing-analysis/id6499280777). One reviewer says it "takes your swing and gives you custom drills" for your issues — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mustard-golf-swing-analysis/id6499280777?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone) (search summary)
- Me and My Golf: an ability-based roadmap suggesting coaching and practice videos and challenges, plus "Shot Fixer" — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/me-and-my-golf-coaching-app/id1541212755)
- V1: pose detection draws skeleton overlays and finds swing frames automatically — [GOATCode (competitor)](https://goatcode.ai/v1-golf-app-review-2026-honest.html) (search summary)
- Rapsodo: Swing Vision and Impact Vision video alongside launch data; "Multi-Angle Swing Vision" and "Rapsodo Combine" marked "Coming Soon"; practice modes include Bag Mapping and Wedge Matrix — [Rapsodo UK](https://rapsodo.co.uk/pages/rapsodo-golf-memberships)

### Inferences
- A "Shot Fix" entry point (start from the miss, not the body position) shows up in both GolfFix and Me and My Golf. It suits average golfers better than starting with angles.
- Reports, missions and goals are how AI apps turn a one-off analysis into a habit.

### Gaps
- No technical detail on how many faults GolfFix or Mustard check, so they can't be compared with Caddy Coach's ~40 checks.

## 3. What makes users return

### Takeaway
People come back for personalised plans and progress tracking (goals, reports, roadmaps), new content added often, community features and competitions, and, for launch monitors, simulated courses and tournaments.

### Cited Findings
- GolfFix: daily and monthly reports with missions, cumulative stats and a global community — [golffix.io](https://www.golffix.io/en/golffix)
- Sportsbox: goal setting, progress tracking and swing comparison over time — [sportsbox.ai](https://www.sportsbox.ai/)
- Me and My Golf: personalised roadmap, new videos weekly, community challenges and a members' community — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/me-and-my-golf-coaching-app/id1541212755)
- Garmin Golf membership: Home Tee Hero virtual courses and a weekly tournament — [PlayBetter](https://www.playbetter.com/blogs/golf-simulator-reviews/garmin-approach-r10-review-golf-launch-monitor-under-1000) (search summary)
- Rapsodo Core: 30k simulated courses and 10,000 saved shots; Studios adds a 4K course library and on-course practice — [Rapsodo UK](https://rapsodo.co.uk/pages/rapsodo-golf-memberships)
- Pinpoint: tracking strokes-gained progress over time — [pinpoint.golf](https://www.pinpoint.golf/) (search summary)

### Inferences
- Caddy Coach already has two-week focus and practice plans. Adding a visible streak or mission and a monthly "swing report" would match what keeps GolfFix and Sportsbox users coming back.

### Gaps
- No retention or engagement figures found for any app.

## 4. Monetisation and current prices

Consumer AI swing apps cost about £50–£150 a year (Mustard UK: £24.99 a month or £149.99 a year, with cheaper £9.99/£49.99 tiers also listed; Sportsbox about $110 a year). Content academies cost about $200–$300 a year. Launch-monitor memberships cost £160–£350 a year. Coach platforms charge coaches about $300–$900 a year. Freemium with a small free allowance is standard.

## 5. What users love and complain about

### Takeaway
Users like accurate fault-spotting and drills that match their faults. They complain about price (especially Mustard at £25 a month), slow processing, only one camera angle, generous scoring, app crashes, and tools that give numbers without telling them what to do.

### Cited Findings
- Mustard (UK reviews): "picks out certain faults in your swing quite accurately"; but "25 pounds a month though is ridiculous", "Price is where this app majorly falls down!!", "very limited" until it can analyse face-on swings, and a request for a free trial with Golf Fix suggested as a cheaper option — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/mustard-golf-swing-analysis/id6499280777)
- Mustard (US reviews): only down-the-line (DTL) analysis and analyses "take a REALLY long time"; "not worth $24 per month"; also "by far the best AI app out there" — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mustard-golf-swing-analysis/id6499280777?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone) (search summary)
- GolfFix: rated 4.8 from 825 ratings in one App Store snapshot (the official site says 4.9). Android users complain recording is fixed at 60fps. The free tier has ads and limited swing history — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/golffix-ai-golf-swing-analyzer/id1586120680?see-all=reviews); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.co.moais.golffix&hl=en_US) (search summary)
- One review says AI grading is too generous for intermediate players, and some Reddit users say the analysis gets stuck at "preparing an analysis at 1%" on longer videos (app not named clearly in the summary) — [Golf Training Daily](https://www.golftrainingdaily.com/blog/ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps) (search summary)
- Sportsbox: "More numbers than verdicts; less useful without a coach to interpret them" — [BirdieSwing (competitor)](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps)
- V1: "a manual tool that will not tell you what is wrong with your swing" — [GOATCode (competitor)](https://goatcode.ai/v1-golf-app-review-2026-honest.html) (search summary)
- Me and My Golf: likes include the coaching plans and fitness plan; complaints include the app closing on launch (2024) and videos not playing (2021) — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/me-and-my-golf-coaching-app/id1541212755)

### Inferences
- Caddy Coach can win on: a free or cheap tier, face-on plus DTL support, fast on-phone processing (no upload wait), and plain-English verdicts rather than raw numbers.

### Gaps
- No direct Reddit threads were retrieved; the Reddit sentiment above comes second-hand. I found no independent accuracy tests of GolfFix or Mustard.

## 6. UX strengths

### Takeaway
The strongest UX patterns are: instant results (GolfFix advertises 10 seconds), a single clear goal and verdict per swing (Sportsbox's goals), starting from the shot problem (Shot Fix), ability-based roadmaps (Me and My Golf), and pro-swing side-by-side comparison with drawn guide lines.

### Cited Findings
- GolfFix: results in about 10 seconds, AI auto guide lines, pro comparison, and a self-record mode with instant replay for practising at the range — [golffix.io](https://www.golffix.io/en/golffix)
- Sportsbox: the free plan gives "1 recommended goal"; paid adds auto swing detection and 2D/3D comparison — [sportsbox.ai](https://www.sportsbox.ai/)
- Me and My Golf: roadmap by ability, 20+ themed plans, Shot Fixer — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/me-and-my-golf-coaching-app/id1541212755)
- V1/OnForm: drawing tools, slow motion, side-by-side, voice-over — [BirdieSwing](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps)

### Inferences
- "One goal at a time" (Sportsbox) matches Caddy Coach's two-week focus. Hands-free self-record with instant replay could be a quick win.

### Gaps
- No hands-on UX testing was done; this section is based on store listings and vendor pages.

## 7. What Caddy Coach could learn; accuracy and limitation claims

### Takeaway
No consumer app publishes validated accuracy figures. Sportsbox is the only one making a strong 3D-from-one-video claim, and even its accuracy FAQ shows no numbers. Caddy Coach can be honest that it uses 2D pose estimation from one phone and stand out on clear verdicts, both camera angles, drills and price.

### Cited Findings
- Sportsbox claims "The #1 Swing Analysis Tool on Tour" and feedback "informed by millions of swings", but its FAQ "How accurate is Sportsbox 3D swing analysis?" showed no figures — [sportsbox.ai](https://www.sportsbox.ai/)
- GolfFix uses "precision analysis" wording but gives no measured accuracy — [golffix.io](https://www.golffix.io/en/golffix)
- A 2026 comparison gives no accuracy percentages for any app and calls Sportsbox "the most quantitative option" — [BirdieSwing](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps)
- Mustard says its AI was built with motion capture and 3D models of thousands of swings — [WBD press release](https://press.wbd.com/us/media-release/golf-digest/golf-digest-mustard-launch-groundbreaking-ai-powered-platform-mustard-golf-help-every) (search summary). Users still report it handles only the DTL view — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/mustard-golf-swing-analysis/id6499280777)
- GolfFix Android recording is fixed at 60fps (a frame-rate limit for fast club movement) — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.co.moais.golffix&hl=en_US) (search summary)
- Hudl Technique shows that even a big brand will shut down or sell a standalone technique app — [OnForm](https://onform.com/blog/onform-acquires-hudl-technique/)

### Inferences
- Ideas Caddy Coach could adapt:
  1. A "Shot Fix" entry from the miss (slice, hook, fat, thin) to likely causes and drills, as GolfFix and Me and My Golf do.
  2. A free tier capped by number of swings (Sportsbox gives 5 a month) instead of hiding features.
  3. Monthly reports and missions (GolfFix) to bring people back.
  4. An AI Q&A over the golfer's own data, as Sportsbox does with SAMI.
  5. Clearly support face-on as well as DTL, the main Mustard complaint.
  6. Avoid generous scoring, which users notice.
  7. Use UK pricing well below Mustard's £24.99/£149.99.
- Single-camera 2D can't reliably measure depth (rotation away from the camera, sway towards it). Caddy Coach should only show checks that the camera angle can actually measure, and say which angle each check needs.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed validation of any phone-based golf pose system was gathered here (outside this task's scope). Golfboy, Rick Shiels, Catapult and Hudl (team) were not covered because of the tool-call limit.
