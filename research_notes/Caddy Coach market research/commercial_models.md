# Commercial models, pricing and unit economics for a UK golf coaching app (Caddy Coach)

All sources accessed 2026-11-18 unless stated. Prices are as shown on the page at that date. Most competitor prices were only available in USD. GBP pricing was not found on official pages (see Gaps). Anything marked **ESTIMATE** is my own arithmetic, not a sourced figure.

## 1. Competitor golf app subscription prices and tiers

### Takeaway
Mainstream GPS/stats apps cluster at **$60–$100 a year** (monthly plans $15–$20), with 7–14 day free trials. Swing-analysis and hardware-linked products charge more: V1 Premium $359/yr, Arccos $199.99/yr after year one. Annual pricing is the norm, and monthly plans are priced to push people towards annual.

### Cited Findings
- **18Birdies Premium**: $99.99/year or $19.99/month — [18Birdies Premium page](https://18birdies.com/premium/). A competitor review also lists a 7-day free trial and notes 18Birdies "runs promotional pricing that varies" — [Golf Pad comparison (2026)](https://golfpadgps.com/news/golf-pad-vs-18birdies-vs-golfshot); [Golfshot blog](https://golfshot.com/blog/golfshot-vs-18birdies-two-of-the-most-used-golf-apps)
- **Golfshot Pro**: $79.99/year — [Golfshot blog](https://golfshot.com/blog/golfshot-vs-18birdies-two-of-the-most-used-golf-apps); [Golf Pad comparison](https://golfpadgps.com/news/golf-pad-vs-18birdies-vs-golfshot) (secondary sources; official Golfshot pricing page not checked)
- **Hole19 Premium**: $69.99/year or $14.99/month, 14-day free trial. Features: plays-like distances, club recommendations, AR (iOS), handicap calculator, stats, shot tracker, watch scoring, no ads — [Hole19 Premium page](https://www.hole19golf.com/premium)
- Hole19 UK discounting (anecdotal): a UK golfer reported "premium for the year was £30 on offer" — [r/BritGolf](https://www.reddit.com/r/BritGolf/comments/1wj79r2/hole19_vs_18birdies_vs_the_grint/) (anecdote only)
- **TheGrint Pro**: $59.99/year — [TheGrint Free vs Pro](https://thegrint.com/features/free-vs-pro)
- **SwingU Pro**: $99.99/year ($8.33/month billed annually); includes strokes gained and a drill after every round — [SwingU blog](https://swingu.com/blog/best-arccos-alternatives)
- **Arccos**: Smart Sensors $249.99 including the first year of membership, then $199.99/year. Arccos Air is $349.99 — [Arccos Smart Sensors](https://www.arccosgolf.com/products/smart-sensors); [SwingU blog](https://swingu.com/blog/best-arccos-alternatives). Reddit users report Costco bundles at $139 that undercut renewal — [r/golf](https://www.reddit.com/r/golf/comments/1hxq4yb/arccos_is_cheaper_to_renew_by_buying_all_new/) (anecdote)
- **Garmin Golf Membership**: Monthly Plan $9.99 USD — [Garmin](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/850633/) (annual price not captured)
- **Sportsbox AI (3D swing analysis)**: "3D Player" from $9.17/month (the excerpt suggests annual billing) — [Sportsbox AI](https://www.sportsbox.ai/)
- **V1 Golf**: Basic $9.99/mo or $69.99/yr. Premium $29.99/mo or $359/yr. Lessons through V1 instructors cost $30–$150. The coach-side V1 Pro is about $499/yr — [Skillest blog, 2026](https://skillest.com/blog/skillest-vs-v1-golf/) (a competitor's blog, not V1's own page)
- **Skillest**: free student account. Coaches set lesson prices from $1–$400 or offer monthly plans. On-demand courses are free to about $50 — [Skillest blog](https://skillest.com/blog/best-online-golf-coaching-platforms-2026/)

### Inferences
- An on-phone swing analysis + GPS + handicap + practice bundle sits between the GPS apps ($60–$100/yr) and V1 Premium ($359/yr). **ESTIMATE**: a UK price of about £4.99–£7.99/month or £34.99–£59.99/year would be in line with the market. The GBP numbers are my own conversion and positioning, not sourced prices.
- Every major competitor offers a free tier plus a paid tier. Trials are 7 days (18Birdies) or 14 days (Hole19).

### Gaps
- No official GBP prices found for 18Birdies, Golfshot, Hole19, TheGrint, SwingU, Garmin or Arccos. UK App Store listings were not checked. App Store GBP tiers usually differ from a straight USD conversion.
- **Shot Scope**: its subscription and app pricing was not found. Its app is believed to be free with hardware, but this is unverified.
- The Garmin Golf annual price and Sportsbox's full tier list were not captured.

## 2. Other golf revenue streams

### Takeaway
Equipment affiliate commissions are modest: about 5–10% for golf retailers and brands, and Amazon UK's Sports rate is unclear. Tee-time booking in the UK is dominated by GolfNow, which runs a barter model with courses rather than paying commissions to third parties. No sourced commission rates were found for lesson marketplaces, BRS/Golfmanager or golf society software.

### Cited Findings
- **American Golf** (UK retailer) affiliate: 6% standard base rate. Sub-networks like FlexOffers quote 1.6–6.4% — [UpPromote directory](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/american-golf/); also 6% fixed — [ReferralRocket](https://referralrocket.io/affiliate-program/american-golf_Mu) (aggregators, not American Golf's own page)
- Other golf brand affiliate programmes: Callaway 6–9% (Partnerize), TaylorMade 5–6% (Impact, 30-day cookie), TGW up to 6.5%, Ben Hogan 10% — [Sports History Network](https://sportshistorynetwork.com/golf/15-golf-affiliate-programs/). Bombtech Golf 10% on Awin — [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/129567). Performance Golf (US) 25% base, 15-day cookie — [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/71491)
- **Amazon UK Associates**: the "EU Fee Statement Appendix" lists Amazon UK "Sports & Outdoor" at a 7.0% fixed standard programme fee rate — [Amazon Associates UK](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/operating/scheduleoct1). That conflicts with the **US** programme, which lists Sports at 1.25% and Outdoors at 0.75% — [Amazon Associates US](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G4ARBJC7Z2NK48CA) — and with reports of Amazon cutting rates by up to 50% — [Hello Partner](https://hellopartner.com/affiliates-face-financial-shock-as-amazons-associates-programme-cuts-commission-rates/). It is unclear whether the UK appendix is the current schedule (the URL suggests an "Oct 1" schedule). **Treat the UK rate as unverified.**
- **GolfNow** (the dominant UK/US tee-time marketplace): its main model is **barter**. Courses give GolfNow tee times, often off-peak, and GolfNow keeps the revenue from those "hot deal" times — [TeeWire comparison](https://teewire.com/teewire-vs-golfnow); [GolfWRX forum](https://forums.golfwrx.com/topic/2003286-golfnow-biz-model/); [NGCOA](https://www.ngcoa.org/golf-business-weekly/2021/june/week-3/golfnow) (US operator body, critical). GolfNow's UK tee-time distribution terms include parity clauses — [GolfNow Business terms PDF](https://golfnowbusiness.com/sites/default/files/2020-04/GOLF-Tee-Time-Distribution-Terms.pdf)
- Coach marketplaces: Skillest coaches set their own prices of $1–$400 per lesson — [Skillest](https://skillest.com/blog/best-online-golf-coaching-platforms-2026/). V1 instructor lessons cost $30–$150 — [Skillest vs V1](https://skillest.com/blog/skillest-vs-v1-golf/). V1 Pro coach software is about $499/yr, which suggests a B2B coach-seat price point (same source).

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATE**: at about 6% commission, a £300 driver referral earns about £18. Affiliate income only matters at volume or for high-ticket items.
- Tee-time revenue is hard for a small app to reach. The incumbent uses barter and parity terms, so a referral or partner link is more realistic than taking a booking commission.
- B2B (coaches, clubs, academies) has a price signal (V1 Pro about $499/yr per coach), but there is no UK-specific data.

### Gaps
- No official affiliate pages found for Golf Clubs 4 Cash or Scottsdale Golf. American Golf's rate comes from aggregators only.
- No published commission rates found for BRS Golf, Golfmanager, Intelligent Golf or other UK club booking systems, or for golf society software (e.g. Golf Genius, society apps).
- Skillest's platform take rate was not found. No UK PGA lesson marketplace commission data was found.
- No sources found on brand sponsorship rates for small golf apps.

## 3. Subscription app benchmarks (RevenueCat 2026, covering 2025 data)

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness, the closest category to a golf improvement app, converts trials well: **median trial-to-paid is 37.7%**. It is mostly **annual**: 68% of plans are annual, with a median of $9.99/month and $39.94/year. Annual plans renew far better than monthly ones. Longer trials convert better.

### Cited Findings (all from [RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps) unless noted; over 115,000 apps, more than $16bn revenue, 2025 data)
- Health & Fitness **trial-to-paid median 37.7%**, top quartile above 51.4%. Western Europe median trial-to-paid across all categories is 29.7%, and North America is 34.2%.
- The 2025 edition put Health & Fitness at a 39.9% median, with the top 10% at 68.3% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- **Download-to-paid** (by day 35) has an overall median of 2.0%. Hard paywall median is 10.7% and freemium median is 2.1% (top quartile above 4.5%).
- **Plan mix**: Health & Fitness is 68% annual.
- **Median prices for Health & Fitness**: $9.99 monthly and $39.94 yearly. The Western Europe App Store yearly median is $40.12.
- **Renewal**: monthly first-renewal medians are about 53–61%, and annual first-renewal medians about 23–40%, varying by category. Active renewal rate is 83.4% for yearly and 39.2% for monthly (as extracted; the definitions differ, so check the report).
- **Refund rates**: median 3–5%. AI apps run at a 4.2% median vs 3.5% for non-AI.
- **Revenue per install**: Health & Fitness is $0.48 at day 14 and $0.66 at day 60. Western Europe overall is $0.25 at day 14.
- **Trial length**: 17–32 day trials have a 42.5% median, 5–9 day trials 37.4%, and trials of 4 days or less 25.5%.
- **Web payments**: web revenue is 3.2% of revenue globally. 41% of top-tier apps earn some web revenue, against 1.3% of hobby-tier apps.
- **AI apps**: 41% more revenue per payer in year one ($30.16 vs $21.37 median). They churn faster: annual retention is 21.1% vs 30.7%, and monthly 6.1% vs 9.5%. They lean heavily towards monthly plans (59.8% vs 26.2%). Note: the extracted AI trial conversion figures (8.5% vs 5.6%) look like a different metric from the category trial-to-paid figures. Check the report before citing them.
- 80–90% of trial starts happen on day 0 — [RevenueCat 2026 summary video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1toNkt5bTgg)

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATE**: out of 1,000 installs, a freemium golf app at the median 2% download-to-paid would get about 20 payers. A hard paywall would get about 100+, though it may cost installs. A 14-day trial, like Hole19's, sits in a reasonable range.
- An annual-first offer fits the category. Golf is also seasonal in the UK, which strengthens the case for annual plans; this is an inference, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No Adapty report data was gathered (not reached within the research budget).
- No golf-specific conversion or churn benchmarks found.

## 4. Platform fees and running costs

### Takeaway
App stores take 15% for small developers. Stripe web payments in the UK cost about 1.5% + 20p, plus 0.7% for Billing. Supabase Pro is $25/month with 100 GB of storage and 250 GB of egress. **GitHub Pages terms forbid using it to run a commercial SaaS**, so hosting must move before charging. On Claude Haiku 5.5, AI coaching costs a fraction of a penny per analysis.

### Cited Findings
- **Apple App Store Small Business Program**: 15% commission on paid apps and in-app purchases for developers with up to $1M in proceeds in the prior calendar year, and for new developers — [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/); [Apple Newsroom 2020](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2020/11/apple-announces-app-store-small-business-program/)
- **Google Play**: 15% service fee on the first $1M of annual earnings. Subscriptions are 15% — [RevenueCat on store fees](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/engineering/app-store-fees); [Chop Dawg](https://www.chopdawg.com/how-to-qualify-for-apples-small-business-program-15-instead-of-30/), which says updated fee structures are rolling out for some regions in mid-2026 (not verified on Google's own page)
- **Stripe UK**: standard UK cards 1.5% + 20p. EEA cards 2.5% + 20p. International cards 3.15% + 20p. Currency conversion 2%. **Stripe Billing 0.7% of Billing volume** — [Stripe GB pricing](https://stripe.com/gb/pricing)
- **Supabase**: Free plan $0 with a 500 MB database and 5 GB egress. **Pro $25/month** with an 8 GB database (then $0.125/GB) and 250 GB egress (then $0.09/GB). Storage overage is $0.0213/GB. Team plan is $599/month — [Supabase pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing). Pro also includes 100 GB file storage, 100,000 MAU and a $10 compute credit — [CloudZero](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/supabase-pricing/). Cached egress via CDN is about $0.03/GB — [Jetadmin](https://www.jetadmin.io/blog/supabase-pricing-2026-guide-to-plans-limits-and-real-world-costs/)
- **GitHub Pages**: "GitHub Pages is not intended for or allowed to be used as a free web-hosting service to run your online business, e-commerce site, or any other website that is primarily directed at either facilitating commercial transactions or providing commercial software as a service (SaaS)." — [GitHub Docs: Pages limits](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)
- **Claude API** (verified on Anthropic's pricing page):
  - Haiku 5.5: input $0.10/MTok and output $0.50/MTok for prompts up to 100k tokens. Above 100k tokens, input is $0.50 and output $2.50.
  - Sonnet 5.5: $2/$10. Opus 5.5: $4/$20. Haiku 4.5: $1/$5.
  - Batch API: 50% off.
  - Cache reads: 0.1x the base input price on most models.
  - Source: [Claude Platform pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)

### Inferences (all ESTIMATES)
- **AI cost per analysis**: an analysis using about 3,000 input and 800 output tokens on Haiku 5.5 costs 3,000×$0.10/1M + 800×$0.50/1M = **$0.0007, about 0.06p**. That matches "about a tenth of a penny". The same call on Sonnet 5.5 costs about $0.014, roughly 1.1p. At 30 analyses a month per user, Haiku costs about 2p per user per month.
- **Stripe on £4.99/month**: 1.5% + 20p + 0.7% is about 31p, or about 6.2%. On **£39.99/year** it is about £1.08, or 2.7%. The fixed 20p fee makes annual billing much cheaper to process.
- **App store on £39.99/year**: 15% is £6.00, about 5.5 times the Stripe cost.
- UK consumer prices include 20% VAT once registered or above the threshold. This is general knowledge and was not sourced here; app stores remit VAT themselves.
- **Video storage**: on-phone analysis avoids most video egress. If clips are uploaded, 100 GB storage and 250 GB egress per month on Pro covers a small user base. Overage is $0.09/GB uncached.
- Native apps add about $99/year for the Apple Developer Program plus Google's one-off fee. Those figures are general knowledge and unsourced here. A PWA plus Stripe avoids the 15% store fee but loses app-store discovery.

### Gaps
- Hosting replacements for GitHub Pages (e.g. Cloudflare Pages, Vercel pricing and commercial terms) were not researched.
- Apple and Google developer account fees were not verified from official pages.
- UK/EU rules on steering users to web payments (anti-steering) were not researched.

## 5. UK golf market size and spending

### Takeaway
England has about **4.2 million** 9- and 18-hole golfers and about **750,000–835,000** registered or club members (2025). GB&I has about 5+ million on-course adult golfers. Green fees and membership costs are rising, which points to a spending-capable audience. No reliable average annual spend per golfer was found.

### Cited Findings
- **England 2025**: 835,554 registered golfers (men 687,942, women 84,162, juniors 63,450). 4,199,000 golfers on 9- and 18-hole courses, registered and unregistered. 2,301 courses. Registered golfers up 6% on 2024 — [R&A / EGA European Golf Participation 2025 (May 2026)](https://ega-golf.ch/sites/ega/files/2026-06/Sporting%20Insights%20Report_The%20RA%20and%20EGA_European%20Golf%20Participation%202025_May%202026.pdf)
- England Golf club membership: 750,071 in 2025, up 2.66% from 730,602 in 2024 — [Floom Creative, citing England Golf](https://floomcreative.co.uk/uk-golf-trends-2026-and-2027/) (secondary source). The difference from the 835,554 above is probably down to definitions ("registered golfers" vs "club members").
- GB&I on-course adult golfers: 5.6 million in 2022, against 3.7 million in 2019 — [National Club Golfer, citing R&A](https://www.nationalclubgolfer.com/club/club/golf-participation-statistics-covid-boom/). A later R&A release cites 5.3 million — [R&A](https://www.randa.org/en/articles/participation-remains-strong-in-gb-i) (the excerpt's year is not clear).
- Global context (outside the US and Mexico): 112.2 million people play golf in all forms, and 43.9 million play 9- or 18-hole golf — [R&A Global Participation 2025 via APGC](https://www.apgc.online/sustained-increase-in-golfs-global-participation/)
- Great Britain 2025 rounds played were reportedly up 25% in Q2 and 23% year to date — [Bunkered (Facebook post)](https://www.facebook.com/BunkeredOnline/posts/golf-is-booming-across-england-more-than-126-million-people-played-the-sport-in-/1720597362886902/) (social post, low reliability). The same post's headline says "12.6 million people played" in England, probably all forms of golf. Not verified.
- Sport England-based figures put adult golf participation in England above 1 million in 2024–25 (range 763,000–1,085,300) — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/899231/golf-participation-uk/) (paywalled, excerpt only)
- **Costs**: the average green fee at the surveyed premium UK courses was £161 in 2021 and £265 in 2026 — [GolfPass](https://www.golfpass.com/travel-advisor/articles/have-golf-course-green-fees-in-the-united-kingdom-gotten-out-of-hand) (a sample of top courses, not typical). Many strong UK and Ireland member clubs charge £700–£1,500 a year — [AmateurGolf.com](https://www.amateurgolf.com/golf-tournament-news/the-true-cost-of-club-life-what-golfers-really-pay-around-the-world-33697). Annual fee rises average 3–7% — [GCMA, citing the Premium Credit Membership Survey 2025-26](https://gcma.org.uk/news/season-tickets-and-golf-club-membership-two-things-it-seems-we-cant-do-without/)

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATE**: about 4.2m England golfers × 2% paying × £40/yr would be about £3.4m a year across the whole category. That is an illustrative ceiling for the market, not a forecast. A realistic early target is hundreds to low thousands of payers.
- A £40/yr app is under 5% of a typical £700–£1,500 club subscription, so price is unlikely to be the main barrier for club members.

### Gaps
- No credible average annual golf spend per UK golfer was found. Sports Marketing Surveys and KPMG figures were not reached.
- Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland figures were not gathered separately.
- The exact current GB-only on-course figure from the R&A was not confirmed.
