# Golf GPS, scoring, statistics and golf-social apps (2025–2026): competitor profiles for Caddy Coach

All sources accessed 18 Nov 2026 unless noted. App Store figures come from UK (apps.apple.com/gb) listings captured on that date. The App Store lists in-app purchase (IAP) names and prices but does not say which price belongs to which plan length, so treat the GBP IAP lists as price points rather than confirmed plan prices. Google Play listings were not checked. No direct r/golf threads were read; the Reddit evidence below is second-hand and flagged as such.

## 1. What each app does and its main selling point

### Takeaway
The market splits into four groups: (a) free GPS and scorecard apps with a paid AI or strategy tier (18Birdies, Hole19, Golfshot, SwingU, GolfLogix, TheGrint); (b) hardware-led automatic shot tracking (Arccos, Shot Scope, Garmin); (c) social, games and society or tournament apps (Golf GameBook, Squabbit, VPAR, ParUp, TheGrint); and (d) UK official handicap and club apps (My England Golf, HowDidiDo). Clippd and Circles are analysis and practice-planning layers rather than GPS apps.

### Cited Findings
**18Birdies.** Free GPS rangefinder, scorecard, handicap tracking, side games and social feed. Premium adds Caddy+ club recommendations, Plays Like distances, 3D green maps, strokes gained and an AI swing analyser. UK App Store rating 4.9 from about 15,000 ratings; version 16.43.0 released in Nov 2026. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/18birdies-golf-gps-tracker/id892700751)
- The company says it passed 10 million cumulative users in June 2026. — [18Birdies](https://18birdies.com/clubhouse/community/10-million-golfers-10-dream-courses-10-weeks)
- Its 2025 year-in-review reports 31M+ rounds tracked in 2025, with a peak of 198K rounds on 3 Aug. — [18Birdies](https://18birdies.com/clubhouse/community/the-18-birdies-2025-community-year-in-review)
- Third-party estimates put monthly active users at 2M–2.5M. These are not the same measure as the 10M cumulative figure. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.squarevalley.i8birdies); [NGF-style report](https://ngf-oracle-reports.pages.dev/usga/golf-app-landscape.html) (unverified mirror site, low confidence)

**Hole19.** Free GPS for 42,000+ courses, scorecard, live leaderboards, Apple Watch GPS and a social network. Premium adds elevation-adjusted distances, shot tracking, club recommendations, AR view, a handicap calculator and dispersion. A higher "Intelligence" tier adds AI caddie round reports, automatic shot detection, strokes gained, handicap benchmarking and CORE Golf drills (iOS). UK rating 4.8 from 14k ratings. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/hole19-golf-gps-range-finder/id794462227)
- Otto AI launched 9 Jul 2025. The release claims 5M+ users in 195 countries and 70M rounds logged. — [First Call Golf press release](https://www.firstcallgolf.com/industry-news/release/2025-07-09/hole19-launches-otto-ai)
- The Intelligence tier was announced late in 2025: Otto writes a report after each round showing strengths, weaknesses and where to focus. This coverage is partly sponsored. — [Hole19 blog](https://www.hole19golf.com/the-19th-hole/hole19-intelligence-tier); [Golf Monthly (sponsored)](https://www.golfmonthly.com/gear/hole19-launches-intelligence-plan-to-help-golfers-lower-scores-faster)

**Arccos Caddie.** Hardware-led automatic shot tracking via Smart Sensors or Smart Grips on every club. Features include an AI GPS rangefinder, AI caddie recommendations, strokes gained, smart club distances and a web dashboard. — [Arccos UK comparison](https://uk.arccosgolf.com/pages/arccos-product-comparison)
- Arccos Air is a pocket or belt wearable that tracks shots without club sensors or a phone, "powered by AI trained on 1.5 billion real golf shots." It includes AI Strategy, Green Maps and Plays Like yardages for slope and weather. — [Arccos Air](https://uk.arccosgolf.com/products/air)
- GolfMagic names Arccos its top pick and cites an Arccos claim that new users cut their handicap by 5.7 strokes on average in year one. That figure is the company's claim. — [GolfMagic, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.golfmagic.com/equipment/buying-guides/best-golf-apps)

**Shot Scope.** GPS watches (e.g. V5) with club tags for automatic shot tracking. Retailers say it gives 100+ statistics, including strokes gained and handicap benchmarking, with no subscription fee. — [American Golf](https://www.americangolf.co.uk/gps/shot-scope-v5-gps-golf-watch-434900.html); [retailer listing](https://www.clubhousegolf.co.uk/acatalog/Shot-Scope-V5-Golf-GPS-Watch-Grey.html)
- Golf Monthly's V5 review calls the strokes gained data "exceptionally useful." — [Golf Monthly](https://www.golfmonthly.com/reviews/gps/shot-scope-v5-golf-gps-watch-review)
- MyGolfSpy reported that Shot Scope added strokes gained course-analysis features. — [MyGolfSpy](https://mygolfspy.com/news-opinion/shot-scope-adds-strokes-gained-course-analysis-features/)

**Golfshot.** GPS with augmented reality (AR). Pro adds auto shot tracking, full Apple Watch features, club recommendations and 3D hole flyovers. A "Champions" tier adds off-course coaching (Golfplan). — [Golfshot help centre](https://shotzoom.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000956554-What-is-Golfshot-Pro-and-what-does-it-cost); [Golf Monthly](https://golfmonthly.com/features/what-is-the-difference-between-golfshot-and-golfshot-pro)

**TheGrint.** A handicap-first app: link a USGA GHIN in the US, or join the GHAP handicap network elsewhere. Also offers GPS for 40,000+ courses, a scorecard with stats, friends, live leaderboards, Skins and Stableford games, and course rankings. UK rating 4.8 from 1.2k ratings; v19.0.26 released 9 Nov 2026. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/thegrint-golf-gps-scorecard/id532085262)

**Garmin Golf.** The companion app for Garmin watches and rangefinders. It offers shot maps, stats, weekly leaderboards across 43,000+ courses and custom tournaments with friends. The paid membership adds Green Contours, Home Tee Hero (virtual rounds with a launch monitor) and swing video storage. UK rating 4.6 from 3.1k ratings. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/garmin-golf/id1192480582)

**GolfLogix.** A 3D GPS app best known for its Putt Line, an animated line that accounts for break, elevation and green speed. It also sells printed Green Books, which are rules-compliant for competition, unlike the app. — [GolfLogix App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id322399059); [Plugged In Golf](https://pluggedingolf.com/?p=77845)

**SwingU (owned by Golf Genius).** Free GPS, scorecard, a handicap after 3 rounds and a daily instruction tip. Plus adds Plays Like yardages, club recommendations, games and Green Maps (13,000+ courses). Pro adds "SwingU Versus" strokes gained, post-round improvement priorities and personalised video drills from top-100 teachers. UK rating 4.7 from about 18k ratings. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/swingu-golf-gps-by-golf-genius/id332116103)

**Golf GameBook.** A social scorecard with 20 game formats, live leaderboards, tournaments for up to 72 players (including Ryder Cup-style team play), a social feed, GPS and its own "GGB Handicap." It claims 1.5M+ golfers, 50M+ rounds and 42,000+ courses. UK rating 4.7 from 1.8k ratings. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/golf-gamebook-scorecard-gps/id409307935)

**Clippd.** AI performance analysis that logs rounds, practice, lessons and simulator golf, scores every shot, and identifies the "skill priorities" that most affect scoring. It has coach accounts and Garmin integration, and its testimonials come from college, top-amateur and LPGA players. — [Clippd](https://clippd.com)

**Circles (Draw More Circles).** A UK developer (Draw More Circles Limited). It is "golf intelligence" stats software with personalised practice plans, trend graphs and practice logs. Free on the App Store. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/circles-golf/id6444925845); [appshunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/6444925845)

**UK-specific apps**
- **My England Golf (MyEG).** The official England Golf WHS app. Golfers pre-register general play rounds, enter scores via "Enter Score" and have them verified by a playing partner who holds a WHS index. — [England Golf help](https://englandgolf.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/80000972978-how-to-add-and-verify-a-score-on-myeg-app)
  - From 1 Sep 2025, clubhouse PSI score entry ended; all scores go through the MyEG app or website. 8.96m scores were submitted in the first eight months of 2025, and Aug 2025 was a record month. — [Northern Golfer](https://www.northerngolfer.co.uk/new-whs-record-set-in-england-in-august/); [GolfMagic](https://www.golfmagic.com/news/england-golf-announces-record-breaking-month-world-handicap-system)
- **HowDidiDo (Club Systems).** Rebuilt and relaunched on 12 Jan 2026. It merges the old HowDidiDo, ClubV1 Members Hub and igMember apps. The dashboard shows Handicap Index, competition entries, bookings and club balances, and score entry has been improved. It claims 1M+ golfers at 2,000+ UK and Ireland clubs. — [National Club Golfer, 8 Jan 2026](https://www.nationalclubgolfer.com/club/howdidido-new-app-charges); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6742023251)
- **iGolf (England Golf).** An official WHS handicap for golfers without a club, used together with MyEG. — [GolfMagic](https://www.golfmagic.com/equipment/buying-guides/best-golf-apps)
- **Squabbit.** Tournament, league and society app with live leaderboards, many formats, season leaderboards, GPS, an Apple Watch app, automatic handicap updates, CSV export and group chat. Covers 30k+ courses in 30+ countries. UK rating 4.9 from 1.2k ratings. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1556538444)
- **VPAR.** Live leaderboards for group events, GPS on 40,000 courses and stats. — [GolfMagic](https://www.golfmagic.com/equipment/buying-guides/best-golf-apps)
- **ParUp Golf.** Free UK app for groups and societies with live scoring, group handicapping and chat. Payments and wagers were still listed as planned. — [GolfMagic](https://www.golfmagic.com/equipment/buying-guides/best-golf-apps); [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/parup-golf/id1546776968)
- **Smaller society apps.** Golf Society Manager (CONGU handicap adjustment, automatic Stableford and strokeplay scoring), RiddyGolf (invites, live scorecards, green-fee collection via Stripe), Teeday and My Society Golf. — [BetaFamily](https://betafamily.com/app/1926); [Golf Business News](https://golfbusinessnews.com/news/new-products/riddygolf-app-aims-to-simplify-society-golf-admin/); [Teeday](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teeday/id6763561922)

### Inferences
- Free GPS plus a scorecard is now table stakes; every major app gives it away. Products now compete on strategy (Plays Like and club recommendations), AI round reports and automatic tracking.
- In the UK, the official handicap loop runs through MyEG and HowDidiDo. Since Sep 2025, every English general play score has to be entered in an app, so golfers already have the habit of entering scores on their phone after a round. The flip side is that third-party apps can't post official scores (see Q5).

### Gaps
- No primary source for Golf Genius's own UK society or club product (Golf Genius as a separate app was not researched beyond its ownership of SwingU).
- No feature detail on "Circles Golf" beyond its App Store listing; it may be a different product from the one the brief means.
- The Golfshot UK App Store page returned 404, so there are no UK rating figures for Golfshot.

## 2. Most innovative features (AI caddie, strokes gained, hardware, social, games, live leaderboards)

### Takeaway
"AI caddie" is now a standard premium label: Arccos AI Strategy, Hole19 Otto, 18Birdies Caddy+/Live Coach and Golfshot club recommendations. The most distinctive moves are hardware-free automatic tracking (Arccos Air, 18Birdies Smart Tracking, Hole19 auto shot detection) and post-round AI reports. The social and society apps compete on game formats and live leaderboards.

### Cited Findings
- **18Birdies Premium.** Plays Like Distances, Club Recommendations, Distance Arcs, Tee Shot Planner, Course Strategy, 3D and heatmap Green Maps, Blind Shot Compass, Live Coach and Live Weather. Smart Tracking (automatic shot detection), Apple Watch swing detection, strokes gained, dispersion, an AI Swing Analyzer, Swing Signature, personalised drills, and Meta AI Glasses support. Help page updated 2 Sep 2026. — [18Birdies help](https://help.18birdies.com/article/661-whats-included-with-18birdies-premium)
- **Arccos Air.** Automatic shot detection from one pocket device, with no club sensors and no phone. — [Arccos](https://uk.arccosgolf.com/products/air)
- **Arccos Smart Laser.** Now includes AI Strategy with its annual subscription. — [Golf Digest](https://www.golfdigest.com/story/arccos-smart-laser-rangefinder-now-to-include-app-access-to-ai-c)
- **Hole19 Otto AI.** Turns round data into personalised insights; the Intelligence tier adds auto shot detection, strokes gained and handicap benchmarking. — [First Call Golf](https://www.firstcallgolf.com/industry-news/release/2025-07-09/hole19-launches-otto-ai); [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/hole19-golf-gps-range-finder/id794462227)
- **SwingU Pro.** Strokes gained ("Versus"), handicap-relative stats and post-round improvement priorities that link to a personalised drill. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/swingu-golf-gps-by-golf-genius/id332116103)
- **Clippd.** Scores every shot on course and in practice, then uses AI to rank "skill priorities." — [Clippd](https://clippd.com)
- **GolfLogix Putt Line.** An animated putt line using break, elevation and speed. — [GolfLogix App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id322399059)
- **Garmin.** Weekly leaderboards across 43,000+ courses, Green Contours and Home Tee Hero simulator rounds. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/garmin-golf/id1192480582)
- **Golf GameBook.** 20 formats, Reds vs Blues team play and 72-player multi-round tournaments. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/golf-gamebook-scorecard-gps/id409307935)
- **Squabbit.** Leaderboard change notifications, season leagues, and "tip" IAPs (e.g. "Tip a beer," £4.99) as a novel way to monetise. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1556538444)
- **18Birdies free tier.** Includes side games (Skins, Wolf, Dots). — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/18birdies-golf-gps-tracker/id892700751)

### Inferences
- The leading "AI" features mostly do one of two jobs: they make a decision on the course (which club, where to aim) or they write a short post-round "what to work on" summary. That second job matches Caddy Coach's coaching angle closely.
- Strokes gained has moved from a hardware-only feature (Arccos, Shot Scope) into phone-only premium tiers (18Birdies, Hole19, SwingU).

### Gaps
- No independent accuracy tests were found comparing the AI caddie or auto-detection features across brands.

## 3. What makes users return

### Takeaway
People come back because of (1) handicap tracking, (2) playing with friends (live leaderboards, games, societies and leagues), (3) round history and trend stats, and (4) post-round insights. The official UK handicap apps get repeat use simply because English golfers must use them to post scores.

### Cited Findings
- Hole19 reviewers credit handicap tracking: "within seven rounds I think I've got my handicap down considerably." — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/hole19-golf-gps-range-finder/id794462227)
- Squabbit reviewers value hands-off scoring for groups: "Just punch in the gross score and it worked out their points." A league organiser says it handled 50–72 players with ease. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1556538444)
- 18Birdies' peak day (198K rounds, 3 Aug 2025) and 31M+ rounds in 2025 show heavy summer use. The company runs community campaigns such as a 10-week "dream courses" sweepstakes. — [18Birdies 2025 review](https://18birdies.com/clubhouse/community/the-18-birdies-2025-community-year-in-review); [18Birdies 10M](https://18birdies.com/clubhouse/community/10-million-golfers-10-dream-courses-10-weeks)
- Golf GameBook reviewers say the stats feedback in Gold "helped their game," and value the social feed and leaderboards. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/golf-gamebook-scorecard-gps/id409307935)
- The HowDidiDo relaunch adds competition-booking reminders and calendar events, giving club golfers more reasons to open the app. — [NCG](https://www.nationalclubgolfer.com/club/howdidido-new-app-charges)
- Since 1 Sep 2025, English general play scores must be entered via MyEG. — [Northern Golfer](https://www.northerngolfer.co.uk/new-whs-record-set-in-england-in-august/)
- SwingU gives free users a daily instruction tip. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/swingu-golf-gps-by-golf-genius/id332116103)

### Inferences
- Group features bring people back because one organiser pulls three or more friends into each round. A single-player coaching app has to create its own reasons to return, such as streaks, practice plans and weekly focus areas.

### Gaps
- No published retention or churn figures for any of these apps were found.

### Gaps

## 5. What users love and complain about

### Takeaway
Users praise accurate yardages, easy scoring and automatic group maths. They complain about (1) high or rising subscription prices and constant upsell prompts, (2) Apple Watch sync and hole-skipping bugs, (3) battery drain and freezing, (4) inaccurate auto club detection, and (5) wrong course data (slope ratings, stroke indexes, missing courses), which matters for UK handicaps.

### Cited Findings
- **18Birdies.** Praised: "the yardages are very accurate." Complaints: the Watch app starts on the wrong hole or skips holes after an update ("absolutely unusable"), and rounds fail to save from the watch. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/18birdies-golf-gps-tracker/id892700751)
- **18Birdies price.** One report says it faces significant pushback over the roughly $100/yr premium price. — [Marlvel report](https://marlvel.ai/apps/18birdies-golf-gps-tracker) (aggregator, moderate confidence)
- **Hole19.** Complaints about slowing response through a round and "it drains the battery rapidly." The reviews shown are from 2018–2023. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/hole19-golf-gps-range-finder/id794462227)
- **Garmin Golf.** "The tracking of clubs is way out." The watch can't detect chips or putts without sensors. "The stats are fairly limited in the app." — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/garmin-golf/id1192480582)
- **SwingU.** Watch stuck on the previous hole, freezing on the first tee, and "purchase premium now messages every couple of days" (2020–21 reviews). — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/swingu-golf-gps-by-golf-genius/id332116103)
- **TheGrint.** Praised for personal customer service and an "intuitive interface." One reviewer found Scottish course and slope ratings "wildly inaccurate" (2022). — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/thegrint-golf-gps-scorecard/id532085262)
- **TheGrint price.** A Feb 2025 user complained the price went from $20 to $60 a year. — [AppFollow / App Store US](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/thegrint-golf-handicap/532085262?country=us)
- **Golf GameBook.** Praised as the easiest golf app the reviewer had tried, with accurate GPS (2025 reviews). Complaints about missing courses and mixed-up stroke indexes (older reviews). — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/golf-gamebook-scorecard-gps/id409307935)
- **Squabbit.** Weak course signal delays leaderboard updates, and its slope settings confused one user. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1556538444)
- **Reddit (second-hand).** An aggregator analysing 490 Reddit reviews rated Arccos 4.6, TheGrint 4.3, 18Birdies 4.2 and Golfshot 4.1. The most common complaints were Golfshot's in-round upsells and Arccos's hardware-plus-subscription cost. Note that Birvix sells a rival app. — [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/golf-app/); [Birvix (biased)](https://birvix.pages.dev/resources/best/best-golf-app-reddit)

## 6. What makes their UX successful

### Takeaway
Successful apps share four habits: a generous free core (GPS, scorecard, handicap), a single obvious premium upgrade tied to the moment of play (Plays Like distances and club recommendations), automatic maths for group formats, and a wrist or no-phone experience (Apple Watch, Arccos Air).

### Cited Findings
- 18Birdies keeps games, leagues, tournaments, the social feed and watch support free, and puts "decision" features behind Premium (Plays Like, club recommendations, strategy). — [18Birdies help](https://help.18birdies.com/article/661-whats-included-with-18birdies-premium)
- Hole19 gives free live leaderboards and wrist yardages. GolfMagic says Premium is needed for club distances, trend analysis and caddie recommendations. — [GolfMagic](https://www.golfmagic.com/equipment/buying-guides/best-golf-apps)
- Golf GameBook calculates playing handicap automatically for each course and syncs scoring, leaderboards and GPS to Apple Watch. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/golf-gamebook-scorecard-gps/id409307935)

## 7. What Caddy Coach could learn or adapt

### Takeaway
Caddy Coach's coaching-first angle (drills, miss guide, two-week focus) is closest to SwingU Pro, Hole19 Otto, Clippd and Circles. Those apps either charge £99+ a year or are US- and college-focused. A UK-first, simple "your round → your one focus → your drill" loop, with UK-correct course data and WHS awareness, looks like open space in the market.

### Inferences (based on the findings above)
- **Copy the loop.** After a round, give a short AI report: strengths, weaknesses, one focus (as Hole19 Otto does), then link it to a drill (as SwingU Pro does). Caddy Coach's two-week focus and drill library already fit this.
- **Keep the core free.** GPS, scorecard and a handicap estimate are free everywhere. Charge for coaching, strategy and insights, priced near £50–£60 a year, under the £99.99 AI tiers.
- **Get UK course data right.** Stroke index, course and slope ratings, and Stableford. This is a recurring complaint about competitors and directly affects WHS playing handicaps.
- **Don't fight MyEG or HowDidiDo on official scores.** Third-party scores aren't accepted. Make it easy to copy a round into MyEG, and show the playing handicap.
- **Use friends and societies to bring people back.** A simple shared live leaderboard with Stableford or Skins for a four-ball or society, with the organiser paying per event (Squabbit model), would add group reasons to return.
- **Avoid known complaints.** Upgrade prompts mid-round, watch and phone sync bugs, battery drain, and unreliable auto club detection.
- **Hardware-free tracking is the trend** (Arccos Air, 18Birdies Smart Tracking). Caddy Coach's shot tracer and pose analysis are phone-only alternatives worth presenting that way.

### Gaps
- No data on how much UK golfers are willing to pay specifically for coaching versus GPS or stats apps.
