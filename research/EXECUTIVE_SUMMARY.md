## The answer in one paragraph

Caddy Coach already has more features than almost any golf app. Swing AI, shot tracer, GPS, the World Handicap System (WHS), club leaderboards and drills are normally spread across three or four separate apps. **What it lacks is one simple weekly loop that brings golfers back, and its best feature, the AI coach, is switched off.**

The fastest route to "one of the best golf apps" is:
1. Switch the AI coach on.
2. After every round, tell the golfer the **one thing** to work on, with the drill and the reason.
3. Make that loop habit-forming with forgiving streaks, partners and societies.
4. Charge a fair UK price, well below the £99.99 that rivals charge for the same idea.

## 10 most important discoveries

1. **Golfers' number-one complaint is price and paywall creep.** FACT: "the £99 sub was hard to justify" ([Golf Monthly forum](https://forums.golfmonthly.com/threads/who-has-moved-away-from-arccos.118414/)); "Most 'free' golf apps gate the important features behind a paywall within the first round" ([ScoringZone](https://www.scoringzone.net/blog/18birdies-review)).
2. **Rivals charge £99.99 for "round → focus → drill".** FACT: Hole19 Intelligence and SwingU Pro both sell this loop at £99.99 a year ([Hole19](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/hole19-golf-gps-range-finder/id794462227); [SwingU](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/swingu-golf-gps-by-golf-genius/id332116103)). Caddy Coach has all the parts and can do it better and cheaper.
3. **Golfers want verdicts, not numbers.** FACT: stats don't "tell me anything significant I don't already know" (Golf Monthly). Sportsbox gives "More numbers than verdicts" ([BirdieSwing](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps), a competitor source).
4. **Every English golfer now opens an app after every round.** FACT: all scores have gone through MyEG since 1 Sep 2025 ([Northern Golfer](https://www.northerngolfer.co.uk/new-whs-record-set-in-england-in-august/)). MyEG is called "glitchy", doesn't show playing handicap and has few stats ([Golf Monthly forum](https://forums.golfmonthly.com/threads/myeg-app-feedback.115847/)).
5. **Tracking hassle makes people quit.** FACT: missed shots, editing rounds and keeping a phone in the pocket drive people away from trackers ([GolfWRX](https://forums.golfwrx.com/topic/2029985-its-time-to-move-on-from-arccos-other-options-for-tracking-rounds/)).
6. **Caddy Coach already beats the leading AI swing app on its main complaints.** FACT: Mustard users object to "25 pounds a month", down-the-line-only analysis and slow results ([App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/mustard-golf-swing-analysis/id6499280777)). Caddy Coach analyses both camera angles, instantly, on the phone.
7. **Habit mechanics from outside golf are proven.** FACT: making one lesson enough to keep a streak raised Duolingo's day-14 retention by 3.3% ([Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak)), and a third of its daily users hold a Friend Streak ([Duolingo IR](https://duolingo.gcs-web.com/static-files/3c8277ee-bc94-4f5d-9b77-0db3e46f88b8)). No golf app in this research uses them well.
8. **The first session decides everything.** FACT: 86.1% of Health & Fitness trial starts happen on install day ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-benchmarks/)). Trials of 17–32 days convert best (42.5% median) ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)).
9. **The AI is almost free to run.** ESTIMATE: one coaching call on Claude Haiku 5.5 is about 3,000 input and 800 output tokens, which costs about **0.06p**. A typical golfer would cost about 29p a year.
10. **Societies and clubs are an open B2B gap.** FACT: societies still use paper and spreadsheets ([Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/golftrips/posts/4424415841106279/)). Squabbit charges organisers per event ([App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1556538444)). HowDidiDo lets clubs pay £1,400 a year to cover all members ([NCG](https://www.nationalclubgolfer.com/club/howdidido-new-app-charges)).

## 5 biggest gaps in the golf app market

REC, each based on the evidence above:
1. **An affordable "what do I work on?" coach.** The round → focus → drill loop exists only in £99.99 tiers or in US college-focused tools.
2. **A friendly MyEG companion.** Golfers must use MyEG and find it glitchy and short on stats. No app positions itself as the helpful layer alongside it.
3. **Honest swing AI.** No consumer app publishes accuracy figures ([BirdieSwing](https://www.birdieswing.com/blog/best-ai-golf-swing-analyzer-apps)), and golfers doubt the grading.
4. **Societies that improve as well as score.** Society apps only keep score; coaching apps only coach.
5. **Habits built for a weekly, seasonal sport.** Golf apps borrow nothing from Duolingo- or Strava-style forgiving streaks, partners and recaps.

---

## 5 features most likely to make Caddy Coach stand out

REC:
1. The **One Thing loop** (debrief + One Focus engine + before/after proof clip).
2. The **AI coach with visible, editable memory**, which only ever sees numbers, never video.
3. The **MyEG companion** plus **stats compared with your own handicap band**.
4. **Honest grading**: checks matched to the camera angle, and a published accuracy test against a PGA professional.
5. **Society mode** with improvement tables, which a scoring-only society app can't copy.

## Recommended subscription model

REC (full working in [Commercial opportunities](./COMMERCIAL_OPPORTUNITIES.md)):
- **Free forever:** GPS, scoring, games, WHS estimate, MyEG card, 3 swing analyses a month, shot tracer, grip and setup checks, drills, streak.
- **Caddy Coach Plus: £44.99 a year or £6.99 a month, with a 21-day trial.** Plus covers the unlimited AI coach, the full debrief, the One Focus engine, handicap-band stats, roadmaps, the monthly report and the full Year in Golf recap.
- **Founding members keep £34.99 a year** for as long as they stay subscribed. This honours the price already written in the code.
- **Never move a free feature behind the paywall.** Never show an upgrade prompt during a round.
- **Sell through Stripe on the web first.** ESTIMATE: Stripe takes £1.19 on £44.99, against £5.62 for an app store.
- **Later additions:** Society Pass (£4.99 per event or £39.99 per season), coach seats (about £99 a year), and paid PGA-pro swing reviews.

## Biggest risks and opportunities

**Risks:**
- **Running costs aren't known yet.** Licence and hosting prices were not researched, so the break-even point is a range. ESTIMATE: 29–171 paying golfers, for fixed costs of £1,000–£6,000 a year.
- **AI apps lose subscribers faster.** FACT: annual retention for AI apps is 21.1%, against 30.7% for non-AI apps ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)).
- **Trust in swing accuracy is fragile.** Grading that is too generous gets noticed.
- **One developer, very large code files.**
- **Golf is seasonal.** Winter churn will hit renewals.

**Opportunities:**
- **A big, growing audience.** FACT: England has 4.2m golfers ([R&A/EGA](https://ega-golf.ch/sites/ega/files/2026-06/Sporting%20Insights%20Report_The%20RA%20and%20EGA_European%20Golf%20Participation%202025_May%202026.pdf)), and club membership grew to 750,071 ([England Golf](https://www.englandgolf.org/news-detail?newsarticleid=1416)).
- **PGA pros and societies as distribution.** One coach or one society organiser brings many golfers with them.
- **Free marketing from shares.** Tracer reels and a December Year in Golf recap.

**Biggest evidence gap:** there is no data broken down by handicap level. Survey the testers.

## What we should build next

**REC: switch on the AI coach and build the post-round "One Thing" debrief on top of it. Put the MyEG companion card on the same screen.**

Why this first:
- It reuses parts the app already has.
- It answers golfers' biggest complaint about stats.
- It matches what rivals charge £99.99 for.
- It arrives at the moment golfers already have their phone out.
- ESTIMATE: it costs about 0.06p per round to run.

A three-week plan is in the [Product roadmap](./PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md#what-we-should-build-next). Measure success with the owner dashboard's 4-week return rate, recorded before and after the change.
